# Privacy Policy (SplitForge)

Effective date: 2026-02-05

SplitForge is a local-first split planner. We do not collect personal data, analytics, or advertising identifiers.

## Data we store
- Training templates, athlete profiles, and plans are stored only on your device.
- Purchase status is cached locally to enable Pro features offline.

## Data we do not collect
- No accounts
- No analytics
- No ads
- No third-party tracking

## Purchases
In-app purchases are handled by the platform store and RevenueCat. SplitForge does not receive your payment details.

## Contact
If you have questions, contact us at support@splitforge.app.
